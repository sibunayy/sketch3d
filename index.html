    Presets

    Start blank, or pick a ready-made body to build on. Picking one replaces your model (Undo brings it back).

    How it works

    1. Start. Tap Presets to start blank or from a ready-made body, then draw your own shapes with the tools on the left.
    2. Change a part. Tap it, then use the panel on the right for roundness, thickness and turning. Paint it with Fill and Colour.
    3. Shape the side. Switch to Side at the top of the drawing. Pull an edge, or open Reshape options for ready-made side shapes like Belly or Waist.
    4. Move it anywhere. Drag a part in Front or Side view, or use the arrow keys (hold Shift for big steps). In 3D, use Move.
    5. Look in 3D. Drag the 3D view to spin it. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
    6. Make it yours. Parts keep hard edges. Turn on Melt into touching parts for a part to blend it smoothly. Crease carves a groove, Hole and Slot cut right through.
    7. Final details. Tap Sculpt to add bumps, dents and smooth spots right on the 3D model. Do this last: sculpting stays where it is if you move parts later.
    8. Print it. Tap Ready to print to check, then Save to print.
